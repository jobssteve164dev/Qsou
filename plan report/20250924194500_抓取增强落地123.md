## 3. 技术方案
- 依赖：`scrapy-playwright`, `playwright`, `trafilatura`, `spidermon`, `scrapy-deltafetch`
- settings 关键项：
  - `TWISTED_REACTOR = "twisted.internet.asyncioreactor.AsyncioSelectorReactor"`
  - `DOWNLOAD_HANDLERS["http|https"] = ScrapyPlaywrightDownloadHandler`
  - `EXTENSIONS["scrapy.extensions.deltafetch.DeltaFetch"] = 100`, `DELTAFETCH_ENABLED=True`
  - `EXTENSIONS["spidermon.contrib.scrapy.extensions.Spidermon"] = 500`
- 监控：定义 `ItemCountMonitor`、`FailureRateMonitor`、`RequiredFieldsMonitor`
- 插件示例：`example_yicai` 打开 JS 渲染并用 trafilatura 提取正文
